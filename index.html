Swarms Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Swarms is a mobile game made by Youssef Gaieb ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email gaaiebyoussef@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and I never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The game shows ads and uses analytics. The first time you play, it asks whether you allow personalised ads and analytics, and you can change that answer later in Settings.
  • I don't sell your personal information myself. Some US state laws count sharing data with ad networks as a "sale", and you can turn that off with "Sell my data" in Settings.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads and analytics: answer the privacy question when the game first starts, or change it any time with "Personalised ads" in Settings. If you refuse, analytics are not collected and ads are not personalised. You may still see non-personalised ads.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • No Ads: buying "No Ads" removes forced ads. Optional reward ads stay available if you want them.
  • Leaderboards: you don't have to use them. Your display name is shown publicly to other players, so don't use your real name if you'd rather not.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes your save from your device. To have your analytics and leaderboard data deleted, email gaaiebyoussef@gmail.com with your leaderboard name, if you have one. I'll ask Unity to delete it within 30 days. For data held by ad networks, use the opt-outs in their own privacy policies.

Third-party services

These services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (Analytics, LevelPlay, Gaming Services, In-App Purchasing): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy
  • The ad networks used through LevelPlay are listed at developers.is.com

Children

Swarms is meant for players aged 13 and older and isn't directed at children. I don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given information through the game, email me and I'll have it deleted.

Security and storage

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit. It may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, by the services named above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how it's used. Email me to make a request. Residents of the EU and UK can also complain to their local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
